        How the score works

        This is a deterministic self-assessment, not AI inference or a probability of success. Each readiness answer is 0–4. Goal clarity and data quality carry 25 points each, data access 20, and ownership and pilot capacity 15 each. Add weight × answer ÷ 4, then round the total.

        Below 40: prepare foundations. From 40 to 69: run a small pilot. From 70: ready for a focused pilot. Your selected goal orders the three ASF tool recommendations. The two lowest readiness answers determine the first actions.

        Results are informational, not a guarantee. Source projects needing a backend are not recommended as browser-hosted tools.
