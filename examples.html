Batch 100 / worked scoring examples

Three goals. Three honest starting points.

Scored answer order: clarity, quality, access, owner, capacity. Context changes advice, not points. Each example opens a populated questionnaire and full local report; no answers are submitted.

Explore a dataset / 49 out of 100

Answers: 2, 1, 3, 2, 2. Arithmetic: 12.5 + 6.25 + 15 + 7.5 + 7.5 = 48.75 → 49. Band: small pilot.

Quality has the largest weighted gap, 18.75 points. Start in Studio, review quality in Analytics, then visualize. Synthetic CSV, a personal audience, and today produce a 15-minute learning plan—not production rollout.

Assess quality / 74 out of 100

Answers: 3, 2, 4, 3, 3. Arithmetic: 18.75 + 12.5 + 20 + 11.25 + 11.25 = 73.75 → 74. Band: focused pilot.

Analytics comes first. Quality remains weakest despite the higher overall score. Flat JSON, team audience, this week, and approved non-sensitive data produce a one-hour validation/review plan. Completeness is not accuracy.

Present a pattern / 93 out of 100

Answers: 4, 4, 4, 3, 3. Arithmetic: 25 + 25 + 20 + 11.25 + 11.25 = 92.5 → 93. Band: focused pilot.

Visualizer comes first, followed by Analytics. Ownership and capacity remain improvement areas. Stakeholder context adds units, exclusions, a decision owner, and an accessible caption. A high score is not proof or permission.

Try a safe counterexample

  1. Open the 93-point example.
  2. Change data status to sensitive, confidential, or unapproved. Old results and drafts disappear.
  3. Regenerate: the score remains 93, but a synthetic-only warning appears.
  4. Copy/download the complete report; email is never required.
